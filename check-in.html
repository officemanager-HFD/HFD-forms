How our check-ins work

Check-ins are a two-way conversation. They exist to improve how we work together, give you feedback you can act on, and make sure expectations are clear on both sides — no surprises. They are not a compensation discussion. Pay increases are tied to core value alignment and results, and are handled separately.

The rhythm: 15–30 minutes, every 4–6 weeks. The team member schedules the check-in from the leadership schedule. If you'll be off on the day of your scheduled check-in, it's on you to switch with another team member and let leadership know so the calendar gets updated.

What we ask of the team member

  1. Schedule your check-ins from the leadership schedule your leader provides.
  2. Be on time.
  3. Fill out this check-in agenda and get it to your leader one day before you meet.
  4. Come ready to run the meeting from your agenda.

What we ask of the leader

  1. Provide the check-in schedule and keep it updated.
  2. Be on time.
  3. Let the team member lead the conversation.
  4. Offer support and give feedback when it's needed.
